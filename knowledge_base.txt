1. ABOUT CAREERPRO MINISTRY

Q: What is CareerPro Ministry?

A: Welcome to CareerPro Ministry, The Elevation Church, Ogba.

CareerPro Ministry is focused on helping believers grow, thrive and make kingdom impact in their careers, businesses and professional lives.

We believe the marketplace is one of the largest mission fields because believers spend a significant part of their lives at work and in business.

CareerPro exists to equip believers to function as Spirit-filled ambassadors of Christ and Priests in the marketplace, combining faith, excellence, wisdom, integrity and professional competence.

Our goal is to help members recognise that their workplace, profession and business are also places where they can demonstrate the character, wisdom and power of God.

2. CAREERPRO VISION

Q: What is the vision of CareerPro Ministry?

A: Our vision is to equip and empower believers in the marketplace to operate with the power, wisdom and guidance of the Holy Spirit in their workplaces, industries, businesses and professional spheres.

We want to see believers become kingdom ambassadors who influence their industries through excellence, integrity, wisdom, leadership and the power of God.

3. CAREERPRO CORE OBJECTIVES

Q: What are the objectives of CareerPro Ministry?

A: CareerPro Ministry aims to:

Activate believers to function as Spirit-filled ambassadors of Christ in the workplace.

Create platforms for shared wisdom, testimonies and mentorship among professionals.

Cultivate a culture of prayer, spiritual authority and ethical leadership in the marketplace.

Equip members with spiritual and practical tools for kingdom influence.

Provide career and business development opportunities.

Support entrepreneurs through funding and business development initiatives.

Help members make informed career and business decisions.

5. MARKETPLACE POWER HOUR

Q: What is Marketplace Power Hour?

A: Marketplace Power Hour is our monthly prayer activation for professionals, entrepreneurs and everyone trusting God for direction and growth in the marketplace.

It is designed to spiritually activate and commission us at the beginning of each month to operate with clarity, wisdom, boldness, favour and divine guidance.

Q: When is Marketplace Power Hour?

A: Marketplace Power Hour takes place on the first working day of every month from 5:00 AM to 6:00 AM.

It is an online prayer session.

Q: Do we fast during Marketplace Power Hour?

A: Yes. We fast on the day of Marketplace Power Hour as part of our spiritual preparation and activation.

Q: What do we pray about during Marketplace Power Hour?

A: Prayer focuses include:

Divine wisdom for career and business decisions

Favour and strategic opportunities

Ethical leadership

Career growth

Business growth

Influence and kingdom impact

Protection in the workplace and business

Divine guidance

Clarity concerning career and business direction

Q: What is the structure of Marketplace Power Hour?

A:

5 minutes — Scripture charge

45 minutes — Strategic prayer

10 minutes — Prophetic declarations over the month

11. CAREERPRO ENTERPRISE DEVELOPMENT FUND

Q: What is the CareerPro Enterprise Development Fund?

A: The CareerPro Enterprise Development Fund is an initiative powered by CareerPro Ministry, The Elevation Church, Ogba, created to support business owners and entrepreneurs with funding to grow and strengthen their businesses.

The fund is part of CareerPro Ministry's commitment to providing practical support and opportunities for people in the marketplace.

Q: How much is the CareerPro Enterprise Development Fund?

A: For the 2027 funding cycle, CareerPro Ministry plans to give out a total of ₦20 million to 10 businesses.

Each selected business will receive ₦2 million.

Total Fund: ₦20,000,000

Number of Businesses: 10

Amount Per Business: ₦2,000,000

Q: Who is the Enterprise Development Fund for?

A: The fund is intended to support eligible business owners and entrepreneurs who meet the requirements for the 2027 CareerPro Enterprise Development Fund.

17. FUNDING SAFETY

Q: How do I know if a grant opportunity is legitimate?

A: Before applying for any grant:

Check the organisation's official website.

Confirm that the opportunity appears on its official communication channels.

Check the application deadline.

Check the eligibility requirements.

Never pay someone simply because they claim they can guarantee you a grant.

Do not share your banking PIN, OTP or password.

Be careful with links sent through unofficial WhatsApp groups.

Where possible, apply directly through the official organisation's website.

CareerPro shares opportunities for information purposes and does not guarantee selection or funding.
